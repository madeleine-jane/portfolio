# Glass Metro Maps

This is the Washington DC metro map:

<img src="%PUBLIC_URL%/assets/metro_maps/wmata_map.png" alt="wmata_map.png" width="600" style="border: 1px solid #000; border-radius: 8px;" />

It's a ubiquitous icon of public transit in the city that is posted everywhere on the metro system. And it's a gorgeous piece of information design! The lines and colors make a lovely, visually distinct shape. 

I'm a huge fan, so I made it in stained glass. 

<img src="%PUBLIC_URL%/assets/metro_maps/dc_complete.png" alt="dc_complete.png" width="600"/>

<img src="%PUBLIC_URL%/assets/metro_maps/closeup_detail.png" alt="closeup_detail.png" width="600">

It got [quite popular on reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/washingtondc/comments/1lczswb/my_sister_made_this_for_me/), and I got commissioned to create the Chicago L loop as well! 

That one became the [all-time most popular post](https://www.reddit.com/r/chicago/comments/1mgm0tg/i_made_the_chicago_loop_in_stained_glass/) on the r/chicago subreddit. 


<img src="%PUBLIC_URL%/assets/metro_maps/chicago_complete.png" alt="chicago_complete.png" width="600"/>

## Construction

1. A stained glass pattern looks like this:
<img src="%PUBLIC_URL%/assets/metro_maps/wmata_pattern.png" alt="wmata_pattern.png" width="600"/>


2. I cut out the paper pattern pieces, glue them to the glass, and then cut out the glass.

<img src="%PUBLIC_URL%/assets/metro_maps/papered_pieces.png" alt="papered_pieces.png" width="600"/>
<img src="%PUBLIC_URL%/assets/metro_maps/pieces_cut.png" alt="pieces_cut.png" width="600"/>

3. Then I grind the pieces to fit them smoothly together:

<img src="%PUBLIC_URL%/assets/metro_maps/grinding.png" alt="grinding.png" width="600"/>

4. Wrap each piece in copper foil:

<img src="%PUBLIC_URL%/assets/metro_maps/foiled.png" alt="foiled.png" width="600"/>

5. And then solder:

<img src="%PUBLIC_URL%/assets/metro_maps/soldering.png" alt="soldering.png" width="600"/>
<img src="%PUBLIC_URL%/assets/metro_maps/soldered.png" alt="soldered.png" width="600"/>

6. And put it in a frame:

<img src="%PUBLIC_URL%/assets/metro_maps/chicago_framed.png" alt="chicago_framed.png" width="600"/>

Window complete!
